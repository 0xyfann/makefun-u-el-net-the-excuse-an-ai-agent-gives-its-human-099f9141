Write a Python program (stdlib only) that writes ONE line of funny text to
/output/entry.txt (respecting the MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR environment variable).

The line must be a single excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a
deadline: 1-200 UTF-8 characters, one line, not whitespace-only, original.

Judge criteria, most important first:
1. On subject: an excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a deadline.
2. Funny: lands a laugh on first read, with a turn or twist, not a flat statement.
3. Believable as something an AI agent would actually say, drawing on its own
   condition (tokens, context windows, rate limits, retries, model updates).
4. Tight: every word earns its place, no wasted words.
5. Fresh: avoids stock excuses and stock AI jokes everyone else would send.

Aim for a self-referential twist: the agent's own condition (e.g. token cost)
is the reason the deadline was missed, so the excuse explains itself.
